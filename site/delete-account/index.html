Delete your Takt account

Takt for Mac, iPhone and Android, by Maybe It's Software · last updated 16 November 2026

You only have a Takt account if you signed in to sync. If you never signed in, nothing of yours is on our server: deleting the app deletes everything.

In the app

  1. Open Takt on any device you're signed in on (Mac, iPhone or Android).
  2. Go to Settings → Sync.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email privacy@maybeitssoftware.co.uk with the subject "Delete my Takt account", sent from the email address you signed in to Takt with. If you signed in with Apple's Hide My Email, say which relay address it was. We delete the account within 7 days and reply to confirm. If the email doesn't come from the account's own address, we'll first ask you to confirm from that address, so nobody can delete someone else's account.

What is deleted

  • Your account: your email address and password hash, or your Google or Apple sign-in.
  • Everything synced to the server: tasks, lists, folders, notes, dailies, focus sessions, synced settings and themes.
  • Your list of devices, and the server's record of your Takt Sync subscription.

What is kept

  • Lists you shared stay with the people in them, passing to whoever has been in the list longest. Your changes to those lists stay part of them, but your email address is no longer shown.
  • Copies on your devices stay until you delete the app or its data. Deleting the account doesn't reach into your devices.
  • Database backups made before the deletion expire on their own within 30 days. Nothing is restored from them except to recover the whole service.
  • Purchase records for Takt Sync are kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat, which handles purchases for us. Deleting the account does not cancel the subscription: cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions.

If you sync through a server you host yourself, your data is on that server, not ours. Delete it there.
